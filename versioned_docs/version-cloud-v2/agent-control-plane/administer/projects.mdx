# Projects

A project groups agents, the MCP servers registered for it, and the environments they're deployed to. Your organization starts with a **Default Project**. For how projects work, see [Project](../../concepts/project.mdx).

## See and add projects

At the organization level, click **Projects** in the left navigation. The **All Projects** page lists your projects; search them by name. To add one, click **Add project**. You can also click **Create project** on the organization's **Overview** page.

## The project overview

Open a project from the breadcrumb at the top, then click **Overview**. The page shows:

- The project's agents, with their **Name**, **Type** (for example, **Platform · Chat**), and when they were **Created**. Search them, or click **New agent** to [add one](../create/add-an-agent.mdx).
- **Agent Deployments**: how many agents are **External** and how many are **Platform** hosted.
- **Project Environments**: the project's deployment pipeline and its environments. The **Default Pipeline** is a standard deployment pipeline with development, staging, and production environments. Click **Change** to use a different pipeline. See [Deployment Pipeline](../../concepts/deployment-pipeline.mdx) and [Environment Management](../../guides/environment-management.mdx).

Click **Manage project** to manage the project's settings.
